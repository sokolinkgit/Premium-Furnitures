Good to know

Questions, answered

Delivery, payment, custom orders and warranty — the things people ask us most.

Ask something else

Yes — living room, dining and kitchen, bedroom and mattresses, wardrobes and storage, office and study, outdoor and garden, kids and baby, décor, plus hotel, restaurant, church and institutional pieces. If it is furniture, we either build it in our Kiambu workshop or source it and finish it to the same standard.

Delivery is countrywide. Within Kiambu and Nairobi we deliver in 1–3 days; upcountry takes 2–5 days by courier or bus parcel. Fees depend on distance and size — small items are usually KSh 500–1,500, and large sets are quoted per trip. The exact cost is confirmed on WhatsApp before you pay anything.

That is most of what we do. Send us a photo, a Pinterest link or a rough sketch with your measurements. We will send fabric swatches, a fixed quote and a production timeline, usually within 24 hours.

For in-stock items, payment is on delivery or on confirmation. For made-to-order pieces we take 50% to start production and the balance on delivery. M-Pesa, bank transfer and cash are all accepted, and every order gets a written receipt.

Every piece carries a 1-year workmanship warranty; mattresses run 3–10 years depending on the model, and frames are built for a decade of daily use. If something fails because of how we built it, we repair or replace it.
