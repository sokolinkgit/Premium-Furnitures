10+ Years of making

Our story

Started with one sofa. Still obsessed with the details.

We began as a two-person carpentry bench in Kiambu, building sofas for neighbours who could not find anything solid at a fair price. Word spread the way it does here — one sitting room at a time.

Today the workshop handles every furniture variety a space needs: living and dining, bedrooms and mattresses, wardrobes, offices, outdoor pieces, kids’ rooms, décor, and full hotel or institutional fit-outs. Same bench. Same standard.

The difference is not a showroom full of imported stock. It is that we can change a dimension, swap a fabric, reinforce a joint or match a wood tone — because we are the ones building it.

What we stand for

Four rules we do not bend

They decide which timber we buy, how long we dry it, and what we tell you when something is not ready.

Built properly, or not at all

Kiln-dried hardwood frames, real joinery, high-resilience foam. The parts you cannot see are the parts that decide whether a sofa lasts three years or fifteen.

Made for your actual room

Millimetres matter. We will happily make a piece 6cm narrower so it clears your doorframe, and we will tell you if a design will not work in your space.

Honest prices, written down

No “call for price”. Every quote is fixed, itemised and confirmed on WhatsApp before production starts. If our cost changes, that is our problem, not yours.

We answer after delivery too

Warranty is a real number, not a sticker. If a hinge sags or a seam opens, send a photo and we will fix it — that is how a workshop earns repeat customers.

Delivery

1–3 days within Kiambu and Nairobi. Countrywide in 2–5 days by courier, bus parcel or our own vehicle for large sets.

Payment

M-Pesa, bank transfer or cash. In-stock pieces on delivery; made-to-order starts at 50% with the balance on delivery.

Warranty

12 months on workmanship and frames, 3–10 years on mattresses. Repairs are handled in the workshop, not outsourced.

Fitting

Wardrobes, reception desks and fitted storage are measured on site, installed by our team, and adjusted until they close properly.
